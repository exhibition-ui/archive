A new book

The People’s Museum for Prince

Minneapolis 2026

The life and music of Prince Rogers Nelson reached millions. Established in 2018, The People’s Museum for Prince turns the spotlight from the artist to his audience, gathering art and personal stories from around the world about Prince’s deep influence on their lives.

This art book celebrates Prince through art and story, drawing on the museum’s 2026 edition in Minneapolis. A gift for Prince lovers, and for anyone who believes an artist can change a life.

What’s in the book?

A hardcover art book with a purple ribbon: 196 pages from the museum’s 2026 edition in Minneapolis, which marked ten years since Prince’s passing.

  • Stories, artworks and portraits from over 70 participants.
  • Photographs of the exhibitions and gatherings by Daniel R. Pratt, Jill Boogren and Kirk Thoren.
  • Curatorial texts introducing the museum.
  • A mosaic of portraits of all the participants and their artworks, on the cover and in special spreads throughout the book.
  • Art-book printing: full color on satin paper, hardcover, 8.5 inches square.

Look inside

Robbin Lang

Commerce City, CO

Always In My Hair

Pressed flower art · 2025

My love for Prince started in 1983, when I was 14. Visiting a friend’s house, her older brother was playing the 1999 album. I was hooked. A few years later, after moving to Minneapolis, I met my partner in crime for all things Prince in high school. Tami and I did everything Prince-related that we could. In 1987 I moved away, but on a visit back we went to Glam Slam for ladies’ night — Prince would usually come those nights to promote new bands. He showed up with his entourage and passed within arm’s length of us. I actually cried.

After Prince passed and Paisley Park opened as a museum, Tami got a part-time job as a tour guide.

Myc Dazzle

Maplewood, MN

When Doves Cry

Digital artwork · 2024

♪ When Doves Cry

“As an artist, his words live rent-free in my head.”

The album and movie my piece commemorates is so Minneapolis. I lived in First Avenue for several years. Between Prince, Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis, we shaped the sound of the 90s. Add to that how much he advocated for artists and the importance of owning our voices, our brands, our being.

Betsy Shannon

Minneapolis, MN

Shining On

Dyed fabric, seed beads; machine pieced, appliquéd and quilted · 2018

♪ Kiss · ♪ Nothing Compares 2U

“As I worked on it I felt the loss and beauty of him and his incredible talent.”

Using fabric from a Cherrywood Fabrics Prince challenge collection, Betsy Shannon recreated an iconic Prince photograph in multiple shades of purple, then hand-embellished it with approximately 15,000 tiny seed beads.

Kaitlin Frick

Seattle, WA

Paisley Park in Winter (Grace is Close to Home)

Etching · 2016

On a snowy Saturday back in 2013, my former partner Clara and I ran some errands. It was the tail end of a volatile year. For six months I had been in an existential crisis of sorts and wanted nothing more than to move away from Minnesota. When Clara suggested we could hit up the southwest suburbs for our provisions, I lit up. Could we take Highway 5? And stop by Paisley Park?!

A few months earlier, we had visited the purple palace for the first time, as our Prince was beginning to re-engage with fans in the space.
